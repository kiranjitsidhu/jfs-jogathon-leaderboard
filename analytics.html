💡 4 Key Takeaways & Playbook for Next Year

These data-grounded insights will help maximize total fundraising and student participation in future Jag-a-Thons:

1. The "Friday 3-5 PM Cutoff Effect"

30.8% of all donations arrive on Friday, with a dramatic surge between 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM (169 gifts). Playbook: Send targeted email/WhatsApp reminders at 2:00 PM on Fridays right before the weekly cutoff to ride this urgency wave.

2. Corporate Match is the Secret Weapon

Corporate matches generated $9,320 (23.5% of all funds) from companies like Google, Waymo, Apple, and Benevity. Playbook: Dedicate Week 2 entirely to a "Match Week" push with step-by-step submission guides.

3. The $100 Sweet Spot

The $100 – $249 bracket accounted for 33.1% of all revenue ($13,100). Meanwhile, gifts under $25 made up 23% of transactions but only 2.9% of funds. Playbook: Feature $100 prominently as the suggested donation on Zeffy.

4. Upper Grade "Pledge vs. Gift" Gap

Grade 6 had high student participation (59%), but raised only $1,460 ($23/student), while Grade 2 raised $19,924 ($276/student). Playbook: Introduce upper-grade experiential incentives (e.g. Teacher Dodgeball, Principal for a Day) to boost pledge size.
