💡 Strategic Executive Playbook (6 Key Takeaways)

These data-grounded insights will help maximize total fundraising and student participation in future Jag-a-Thons:

1. The "Friday Cutoff & Batch-Log" Surge

30.8% of all donations are recorded on Friday, particularly from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM. This peak combines last-minute online parent urgency with the organizer batch-logging classroom envelopes, cash/checks, and match receipts right before the deadline. Playbook: Set classroom envelope turn-in by Thursday afternoon or Friday morning so organizers have buffer time to log offline gifts before the 4/5 PM buzzer.

2. The Multi-Donor "Flywheel Effect"

72% of students received only 1 donation (averaging $50). But the 14% of students who received 3+ donations accounted for 67.1% ($26,500+) of all money raised. Playbook: The biggest revenue driver isn't asking parents to give more—it's giving them 1-click tools (WhatsApp/text templates) to easily ask grandparents, aunts, uncles, and friends.

3. Corporate Match is the Secret Weapon

Corporate matches generated $9,320 (23.5% of all funds) from companies like Google, Waymo, Apple, and Benevity. Playbook: Dedicate Week 2 entirely to a "Match Week" push with step-by-step submission guides.

4. The $100 Sweet Spot

The $100 – $249 bracket accounted for 33.1% of all revenue ($13,100). Meanwhile, gifts under $25 made up 23% of transactions but only 2.9% of funds. Playbook: Feature $100 prominently as the suggested donation on Zeffy.

5. Upper Grade "Pledge vs. Gift" Gap

Grade 6 had high student participation (59%), but raised only $1,460 ($23/student), while Grade 2 raised $19,924 ($276/student). Playbook: Introduce upper-grade experiential incentives (e.g. Teacher Dodgeball, Principal for a Day) to boost pledge size.

6. The "Sibling Split" Household Effect (Shubhra's Insight)

40 multi-child families (85 students) contributed $9,236 (22.7% of all funds). In 50% of these families, parents split donations exactly equally between siblings (e.g. $150 each across 3 kids, or $250 each across 2 kids). Their true giving power was masked on individual student leaderboards! Playbook: Offer a 1-click "Family / Sibling Pledge" in Zeffy so parents checkout once and split evenly, plus introduce a combined Household Giving Leaderboard.
