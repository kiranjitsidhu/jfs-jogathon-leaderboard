📋 Campaign Guidelines & Important Deadlines

⏰

Weekly Challenge Reset Times

Weekly sprint challenges reset every Friday at 3:00 PM across all four weeks (Oct 19 – Nov 13).

💵

Cash & Check Donation Cutoff

Physical cash and check donations must be submitted to the school office by Friday at 2:00 PM to count toward the following Monday's weekly drawing and raffle prizes.

🏢

Corporate Matching (100% Included!)

Corporate employer matches (Apple, Google, Cisco, Intel, Adobe, etc.) are included in student and class totals!

1. Submit your match to JFS PTA.

2. Email your match contribution receipt to Poonam Sidhu at poonam.jolly@gmail.com.

Emails must be received by 9:00 AM on Friday to count in that week's total. Final deadline for all corporate matches is Friday, Nov 13th at 9:00 AM.

📦

Prize Distribution

Weekly small items (stickers, bracelets, tchotchkes) will be distributed Mondays via teachers. Grand prizes and experience events will be fulfilled the week following campaign completion.

Questions? Contact Fundraising Chair: Poonam Sidhu (poonam.jolly@gmail.com).
