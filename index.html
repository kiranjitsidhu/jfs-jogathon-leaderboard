📋 Campaign Guidelines & Important Deadlines

⏰

Weekly Challenge Cutoff

Weekly sprint challenges cut off at 3:00 PM each week on Friday.

💵

Cash & Check Donations

Physical cash and check donations must be submitted to the school office by Friday at 2:00 PM to count towards the current week’s tally.

🏢

Corporate Matching (100% Counted!)

Corporate employer matches (Apple, Google, Cisco, Intel, Adobe, etc.) are included in student and class totals!

1. Submit your match to JFS PTA.

2. Email your match contribution receipt to Mugdha at muggikale@gmail.com.

Emails must be received by 9:00 AM on Friday to count in that week's total. Final deadline for all corporate matches is Friday, Nov 13th at 9:00 AM.

📦

Prize Distribution Timeline

Small individual weekly prizes (tchotchkes) will be distributed weekly in classrooms. Grand prizes (weekly and overall) will be awarded at the end of the jag-a-thon. All class- and grade-wide experiences will be scheduled with teachers and administrators after the jag-a-thon concludes (we appreciate your patience as parent volunteers catch their breaths!).

🤝

Questions & Prize Sponsorship

We are looking for parents and volunteers to sponsor our incredible prizes this year! For all inquiries (attributing donations, prize tracking, or sponsorship) please contact Poonam Sidhu (poonam.jolly@gmail.com).
